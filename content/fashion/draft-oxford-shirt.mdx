Template entry for a woven shirt. It exists so the fashion section can be
read, tested and reviewed before a real garment is written up, and it is
hidden from every listing, the feed and the sitemap until it is replaced.

Nothing here is a judgement. The fit, the size, the blend, the wash symbols
and the wear rate are placeholders that happen to be plausible for an oxford
shirt, not readings off a label in a wardrobe. Replace every one of them from
the garment itself, add the purchase date to `changelog`, then delete
`hidden: true`.

A shirt is worth reviewing for three things a product page will not tell you:
whether the labelled size matches the body it is cut for, what the collar and
the cuffs look like after twenty washes, and whether the care label is a
suggestion or a bill. All three are fields above, not adjectives.

Template entry for denim. Hidden until a real pair replaces it.

Denim is the clearest argument for treating longevity as data rather than
prose. The same pair is worse than a cheap one at week one and better than
anything else in the wardrobe at year three, and the only way to say that
honestly is to write down when it was first worn, roughly how often it is
worn, and what changed on which date.

The blend, the care symbols, the wear rate and the condition above are
placeholders chosen to exercise the layout. None of them describe a pair of
jeans anyone owns. Fill them in from the label and the leather patch, log the
purchase in `changelog`, and only then remove `hidden: true`.
